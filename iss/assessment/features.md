Assemment dashboards is for a team to access various issues submitted by the public for assessment and further action. The team consists of selected stakholders. they will see the issues after the admin/mp has marked the issue for assessment. The team members can then assess the issue and make recommendations for further action.

they will have access to a dashboard that shows them the issues assigned to them for assessment. They can view the details of each issue, add their assessment comments, and recommend actions to be taken. The dashboard will also show the status of each issue, whether it is pending, in progress, or resolved.

then when the mp gives the go ahead and provides funds to fix the issues, the assesment team would take actions to solve it and provide resolutions comments and upload images/files and notes.
